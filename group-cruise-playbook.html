The ships

Virgin Voyages is adults only, 18+

These 2027 group sailings are planned with Virgin Voyages, which is designed for guests 18 and older. That makes them a fit for couples, friend groups, milestone celebrations, wedding parties, alumni gatherings, and company incentive trips. They are not family sailings, and they are not a traditional faith-retreat product.

How a group trip works

You gather the people. Rhonda runs the details.

The organizer does not have to become the travel agent. Rhonda pre-qualifies the group, helps with cabin choices once fares are confirmed, and closes the booking. Deposits are taken by Rhonda after a consultation, not through a form on this website. Nothing here holds a cabin or quotes a fare that is not yet final.

What to decide first

  • Who is going, and about how many cabins you might need
  • Whether everyone is 18 or older
  • Which direction feels right: Mexico, Europe, or Alaska
  • Passports and timing, once sail dates are confirmed
  • A budget range to talk through with Rhonda on a call
